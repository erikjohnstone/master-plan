# HVAC / BAS Focus 30

Selected from both supplied Google Drive archives. Curation date: 2026-09-04.

**113 logical drawing sets, 190 physical PDF files and 4,799 pages screened. The focus group contains 30 PDFs and 1,489 pages across 17 named U.S. states, plus one set whose location is not established.**

This is a deliberately diverse, evidence-backed development corpus, not a claim of a mathematically optimal selection or a finished takeoff answer key. The order below is a suggested exploration order. Not every PDF contains every requested feature; the matrix and individual notes identify exactly what is present, referenced elsewhere, or intentionally absent.

Selection mix: 20 core, 8 specialist, 2 vector-outline challenge. Twenty-eight selections are native-text-led; two are explicitly separated as vector-outline challenges. Native-text-led does not mean every element on every sheet is searchable. Logos, stamps, reference details and some body text can use different encodings.

## How this was selected and checked

1. Downloaded both supplied ZIP files completely; recorded byte counts and SHA-256 hashes. Enumerated the archive contents rather than trusting the INDEX counts. Volume 1 includes an additional D_25 drawing set beyond its 30-entry index.
2. Grouped contiguous __partNN_pX-Y files into logical deliveries and validated every part range. The total is 113 logical sets, not 190 independent projects. A supplied delivery can itself be an excerpt of a larger project.
3. Read every one of the 4,799 pages with PyMuPDF. Recorded native text, visible/invisible text operations, vector drawing operations, embedded-image areas and keyword hits. The full-page audit is in all_4799_pages.csv. Keyword counts are screening signals only: indices, legends, sample charts and contractor-deliverable requirements create false positives.
4. Investigated high-value candidates using extracted text and page renderings. Visually reviewed at least one substantive evidence page in each selected set; the exact reviewed pages are recorded in manifest.json. This is not a claim that every source page was manually read or every cell transcribed. Selected evidence includes both populated controls anchors and intentionally narrower specialists.
5. Balanced system/application diversity and table structure: hydronic plants, steam, laboratory air valves, ice storage, chemical ventilation, computer-room cooling, preservation humidity, factory integration, electrical/PLC I/O, transposed/rotated/slanted headers, continuation tables, quantity expansion, alternates and scope conflicts. Related delivery phases were avoided where they added little; some related agency/designer families remain explicitly identified.
6. Copied single-file selections byte-for-byte. Rejoined split selections in original page order without rasterizing, downsampling, adding OCR or removing source pages. Checked every rejoined page against its original text hash, dimensions, vector-operation count and image-operation count. Compared both delivered preview renderings for each selected PDF with their source renderings pixel-for-pixel.

**Graphics diagnostics are not a takeoff-quality score.** Vector-path counts measure drawing operations, not individual line segments. Largest-image ratio measures the largest single image, not total raster coverage, and may miss a table-sized raster patch. A vector title block does not prove the table is vector. Outline text can look excellent while yielding almost no searchable characters. These are why content review and two separate text-encoding tracks matter.

## Important exclusions and caveats

- Judson ISD: visually attractive exported points data, but the relevant p. 13 table body is raster, despite a vector sheet border. Not selected for this vector-first group.
- LAMBDA: strong-looking controls material, but an important points-list body is raster. Retained in the downloaded sources, not the selected 30.
- Building 112 Chiller Addition: image-heavy content and schedule mentions that are requirements rather than populated control-valve records.
- Guaranteed Rate Field Phase VIII, additional Lovell delivery and related LBNL portion: avoided extra project-family concentration.
- Ordinary “valve schedules” can list manual isolation valves. Houston VA is retained specifically to test this distinction, not falsely credited with actuator/Cv data.
- Several specialist selections refer to external specifications or existing sequences. Missing information is a valid expected finding; do not fabricate a complete points list, actuator torque, quantity, valve Cv or sequence.
- Archive names/locations are not ground truth. Title-block corrections include Columbia, MO for Truman VA, Houston, TX for the ER set, and Gardnerville, NV for Carson Valley. Original archive metadata is preserved in the manifest for traceability.
- No exact normalized-text page overlaps involving multiple pages per pair were found by the screening audit. This is not a perceptual/near-duplicate audit and does not establish independence of design templates.

## Using this as a serious development corpus

Use the 30 as a focus/development group, not as an already-clean holdout. Cherry Point and ITD District 1 have prior exposure in this workspace. Public plan sets may also have unknown prior model exposure. Do not make a clean-test or generalization claim from them.

Before scoring, create an independently reviewed, page/region-grounded answer key. Preserve evidence provenance and allow “unknown / external document / source conflict” as explicit values. Suggested annotation tasks:

- Schedule structure: table bounds, row/column hierarchy, merged cells, continuation links, units and footnote inheritance.
- Equipment identity: project/building/system/tag, quantities, equipment versus assembly/subcomponent, existing/new/alternate/excluded.
- Valves and dampers: device category, sizing, flow/Cv, signal, power, normal/fail state, feedback, actuator responsibility and unresolved requirements.
- Points: physical I/O versus network integration versus software/virtual points, per-unit versus shared/global points, alarms/trends and controller ownership.
- Sequences: modes, enable conditions, interlocks, timers, setpoint/reset curves, failure behavior and source conflicts. Validate the engineering interpretation separately from text extraction.

Split future development and evaluation by project family and, where feasible, designer/template. Keep related ITD cases and the two JVWTP projects together; do not randomly split their pages into training and test. Keep the two vector-outline challenges separately reported. Do not infer that the 83 unselected sets are untouched test data: they were used in corpus screening and many have related templates.

No autonomous takeoff accuracy, recall or quantity totals were evaluated in this curation task. This package prepares the evidence base; it does not certify a production estimator.
