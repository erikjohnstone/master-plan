## Reading this record

- All twelve supplied pages were reviewed as full-page 180 DPI Poppler renders. MuPDF and Poppler independently corroborate every authored schedule/points cell and bounded text assertion.
- MH601 has seven populated schedules and 69 source rows: 20 single-duct terminals, 1 series fan-powered terminal, 8 air-device type rows, 2 filter rows, and 38 heating-hot-water supply/return BALL VALVE rows.
- The 38 valve rows are literal tagged manual ball valves with service, size and flow. Because the schedule has no actuator, Cv, control signal or fail position, they are deliberately excluded from BAS control-valve/actuator counts.
- MH502's 19-row VAV terminal unit point list is a printed point-function matrix with no terminal equipment-tag column or source binding to MH601 schedule rows. Its listed hardware/software totals are not multiplied into installed I/O or controller-terminal totals.
- MH502 nevertheless contains high-value written behavior: continuous VAV operation, occupied/unoccupied airflow and reheat logic, discharge-temperature/humidity alarms, and isolation-room differential-pressure, HEPA-filter, lead/standby-fan, VFD and fan-alarm sequences.
