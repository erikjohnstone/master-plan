## Reading this record

- All 13 supplied pages were visually reviewed as full-page 144 DPI Poppler renders. The P&ID and schedule sheet received additional high-resolution Poppler crop review. Every authored schedule cell and bounded assertion is independently checked against retained MuPDF and Poppler evidence.
- M7.0's strict equipment component captures two N+1 PCW pumps, one PCW/CHW heat exchanger, two pump VFDs, one compressed-air regulator, one grille row, one relocated reheat coil, one existing AHU reference row and one PCW air separator row. Pot feeder, expansion tank and filter are retained in the source-inventory boundary.
- M6.3 prints 23 typed diagram callouts: 15 AI, two DI, four AO and two DO. It is not a final point/PLC-terminal schedule and it explicitly assigns production of a points list to the I&C contractor, so the record does not manufacture an installed I/O total.
- The controls record preserves the 42 PSIG adjustable differential-pressure control, 63°F adjustable PCW temperature control, continuous dual-pump operation, pump failure response, makeup behavior, defined alarms, Modbus TCP to Ignition SCADA and mirrored EPICS context.
- The M6.3 diagram contains a modulating CHW valve and makeup actuator but the supplied set has neither a true control-valve schedule nor a damper-actuator schedule; unsupported selection data remain unavailable.
