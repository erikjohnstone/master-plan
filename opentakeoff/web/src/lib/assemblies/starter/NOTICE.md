# Starter library v1: sources, licenses and partner review

This folder holds OpenTakeoff's vendor-neutral starter content for the
assemblies engine (`web/src/lib/assemblies/`). The records name device,
component and point *roles* and their parameters. Choosing products, setting
commercial terms and assigning labor allowances are left to partner data;
nothing here carries them.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `us-typicals-v1.json` | 31 BAS typicals (layer `controls`), covering the 26 of research 02 §3b |
| `us-hookups-v1.json` | 16 mechanical hook-up records (layer `hookup`): 5 parts, 10 equipment hook-ups, 1 project record |
| `responsibility-v1.json` | The single responsibility matrix every line uses, and the four coil-kit presets |
| `hookup-profile-v1.json` | The hook-up profile's switches and project variables, each with its default and the sources that disagree |
| `coverage-ufc-3-410-01.json` | Where each item of UFC 3-410-01 Table 3-1 appears, or why it does not |
| `coverage-g36-chiller-plant.json` | Where each field connector of the G36 chiller plant controller appears, or why it does not |

The JSON is generated by `web/scripts/assemblies-starter/build.mts` and
checked by `web/test/assemblies/starter.test.ts`, which rebuilds it and
requires the same bytes.

## For partner review (GATE 4)

These are the choices a controls integrator and a mechanical contractor
should confirm or change before the library is used on a bid.

**Typicals resting on inference:**
- `lab-airflow`: no primary source was read. It is applied only by override.
- `rtu-networked`: the exposed network point list is an inference. The rules
  are UFGS 23 09 23.02 §3.1.5.
- `vrf-indoor` and `vrf-outdoor`: the per-unit network points.
- `erv`, `humidifier` (enable and alarm) and the building-level outdoor
  sensors of `building-meters`.
- The G36 network values (NET-IN and NET-OUT lines): whether to count them
  as points.

**How a typical is chosen:**
- A multizone or single-zone VAV air handler is told apart by the number of
  terminal units that name it (`terminals_served`). Until that count is
  derived (WP5), such a unit is `unresolved` with both candidates.
- An AHU or RTU at 100% outdoor air takes `doas` by override.
- A DOAS whose schedule prints no energy recovery leaves those lines
  unresolved.
- A fan coil unit whose schedule prints no pipe count is taken as 4-pipe when
  it prints both chilled- and hot-water coil data.
- An EC-motor fan with no VFD takes `fan-constant`.
- An RTU that prints a BAS interface takes `rtu-networked`. The attribute is
  free text, so a hardwired interface would also select it.
- These have no typical in v1, so they are listed as "no assembly":
  - exhaust, induction and chilled-beam terminals;
  - the families CONDENSING_UNIT, CRAH, DEHUMIDIFIER, DUCT_MOUNTED_COIL,
    FIN_TUBE_RADIATION, RADIANT_CEILING_PANEL and RAH.

  Radiation schedules list element types, not controlled zones, so a
  per-row recipe would miscount valves.

**Staging and signals:**
- DX cooling is staged only when the schedule prints stages. Otherwise it is
  G36's modulating signal.
- Families without a stage count (furnace, heat pump, unit heater) get the
  first stage only, with a note.
- Electric reheat is staged unless the SCR option is set.

**Responsibility (`responsibility-v1.json`):**
- The VA 23 09 23 table is the default.
- VA contradicts itself on flow switches and flow meters (the table against
  its §1.1 list).
- Smoke detectors follow VA (fire alarm throughout). Some university
  specifications have the mechanical trade install duct detectors.
- Who installs airflow measuring stations and who furnishes the HVAC
  shutdown switch are open questions.
- Packaged-unit controls are assumed factory furnished.
- The four coil-kit presets decide who furnishes the control valve.

**Hook-up profile (`hookup-profile-v1.json`):**
- Defaults:
  - no hoses at terminal coils;
  - kits at 1 in. and below;
  - manual balancing;
  - 2-way valves;
  - flanges from 2 in.;
  - P/T ports and strainers at every coil;
  - thermometers and gauges at air handler coils;
  - access doors at reheat coils;
  - no trap at room fan coil units;
  - no trap bypass;
  - no boiler gas regulator;
  - float makeup at towers.
- The specifications disagree on each; the file lists them.

**Scope switches:**
- `ufc_minimum_points` is off by default. Turn it on for DoD work: UFC
  3-410-01 §3-2.3.4 requires Table 3-1 there.
- Duct smoke detectors are on by default (UFGS 23 09 93 §3.2).

**Labor hooks:**
- Performance verification sampling follows UFGS 23 09 00 §3.7.6: every
  primary system, air handler and DOAS; 20% of identical terminal units,
  exhaust fans, fan coil units and unit heaters.
- Every point and device carries a task and a driver, never an allowance.
